Bhutan Tourism

"Bhutan is one of the most favored tourist destinations in the world and the tourism sector. Which is one of its most vibrant businesses, is growing at a fast pace. Keeping in sync with its moral. Ethical and cultural values, the Bhutan Tourism industry follows a strict policy of allowing a low volume tourism in order to adhere to its image of exclusivity and seclusion. As well as to conserve its cultural and spiritual heritage, and natural environment."

Missions:
  • Developing and promoting tourism sector such that it is economically viable. And is socially and culturally acceptable
  • Developing and creating a vibrant, ecologically-sensitive, and tourist friendly industry
  • Developing infrastructure in order to facilitate sustainability in tourism by making Bhutan a 365-days tourism destination so that the ethics and essence of Bhutanese culture could be spread worldwide
