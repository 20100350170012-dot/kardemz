Paro Tiger’s Nest, or Taktsang Monastery, is a beautiful and historic monastery in Bhutan. It is built high on a rocky cliff surrounded by green forests and mountains. Visitors have to hike up the mountain to reach the monastery. The peaceful surroundings and stunning views make it a special place for both pilgrims and tourists. Tiger’s Nest is considered a symbol of Bhutan’s rich culture, spirituality, and natural beauty.
